ORDERS WIN CONDITION AND ICON/LABEL CORRECTIONS (PHASE 5)
=========================================================

Purpose of this document
------------------------
Three things, all reported from a play test:

  1. The level goal was still the old flat counter, not the order list.
  2. Baked icons rendered as small rectangles in the slots.
  3. The "+1" label had no drink icon beside it, unlike the reference.

1. Win condition
----------------
Before: GameManager.CheckWinCondition ended the level at
GameDataManager.boxNum >= 10, regardless of soda color.

Now: a scene that has an OrderManager with a non-empty order list is won when
OrderManager raises AllOrdersFulfilled. Scenes without one keep the old counter,
so the Tutorial and any level not yet converted still finish normally. That is
what UsesOrderWinCondition decides.

Why CheckWinCondition returns early instead of also checking orders
    AllOrdersFulfilled deliberately fires late. OrderManager waits until every
    travelling effect has landed, so the last slot has visibly ticked to its
    green check before the win panel appears. If CheckWinCondition also ended
    the level on the packed-box count, a level whose orders total ten boxes
    would end at the moment of packing and cover a slot still showing a number.
    Returning early is what guarantees the panel follows the animation.

BeginWinSequence
    Both goal types now funnel through one method, so the end-sequence guard,
    the spawn stop, and the music stop cannot diverge between them.

Subscription timing
    Subscribed in GameManager.Start, not Awake. OrderManager builds its runtime
    list in its own Start, so an Awake subscription would see an empty manager
    and HasOrders would read false. The handler is removed in OnDestroy.

2. Icons rendering as small rectangles
--------------------------------------
Two separate causes, both fixed.

Cause A: the sprite was being stretched
    The slot's Image had preserveAspect off. A soda is much taller than it is
    wide, so a square slot stretched the sprite horizontally into a rectangle.

    OrderSlotUI.Bind now sets preserveAspect and Image.Type.Simple at runtime,
    not only in the builder, so panels created before this fix are corrected
    without being rebuilt. The builder also sets it, and the icon is larger.

Cause B: the drink occupied a small part of its own texture
    The camera framed the model using the bounding sphere radius. For a tall,
    narrow can that radius is far larger than the camera needs, so the can was
    rendered small inside a mostly empty square, and the UI then scaled the
    whole square, empty margin included.

    Two changes:

    Bounding-box framing
        The eight corners of the model's bounds are transformed into camera
        space and the orthographic size is taken from the largest half-extent
        actually seen. That fits the can rather than the sphere around it.

    Margin trimming
        After rendering, the image is cropped to the pixels that are actually
        drawn, then re-squared with a small even margin. This is the change that
        guarantees the result regardless of framing or camera angle, and it also
        makes every color produce identically proportioned icons no matter how
        wide or narrow its silhouette is.

    Alpha is tested against a small threshold, not against zero, so anti-aliased
    edge pixels are kept.

Supersampling
    The bake now renders at up to 4x the icon size and downsamples with a
    bilinear filter. The extra resolution removes the stair-stepping on the
    can's curved silhouette, which was clearly visible at 256 px. Texture2D has
    no resize, and a nearest-neighbour copy would have reintroduced exactly the
    aliasing the larger render removed, hence the manual bilinear pass.

New window options
    Supersample  1 to 4, default 3
    Trim Margin  on by default

3. Icon beside the "+1"
-----------------------
PlayPlusOneLabel now also rents an Image, assigns the drink's baked sprite, and
moves it beside the text.

They are two separate pooled objects driven by the same offset each frame rather
than a parented pair. The pools stay flat, and the icon is optional: when the
library has no sprite for that color, only the text plays and nothing has to be
rebuilt or re-parented.

The pair is centred on the origin, so adding an icon does not shift the "+1"
away from where the box actually was.

Pool reset, and the bug it prevents
    The same pooled Image now serves three roles: comet, sparkle, and "+1" icon.
    Only the icon wants preserveAspect on. Left set, the next comet would be
    squashed to its sprite's ratio instead of filling its rectangle. ReturnImage
    therefore clears preserveAspect and the sprite reference on return, rather
    than each caller having to remember to reset it.

Steps to apply
--------------
1. Rebake the icons: Tools > Coca Sorting > Bake Soda Icons > Bake All Colors.
   The new framing, trimming, and supersampling only apply to a fresh bake.
2. Save the scene.
3. Play. The level should now end when the last order gets its green check,
   after the final effect lands.

Validation performed
--------------------
- Assembly-CSharp and Assembly-CSharp-Editor both compile.
- Scenes with no OrderManager, and scenes with an empty order list, still use
  the packed-box counter, so the Tutorial is unaffected.
- Both win paths run through BeginWinSequence and its single guard.
- The order subscription is removed in OnDestroy.
- Requires the Unity Editor to confirm icon framing and the label layout.

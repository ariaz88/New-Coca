LIFT TRUCK QUEUE AND MOVEMENT SAFETY
====================================

Related scripts
---------------
- Assets/Scripts/Main Scripts/Core Gameplay/LiftTruck.cs
- Assets/Scripts/Main Scripts/Managers/LiftTruckManager.cs

Problem that was fixed
----------------------
The old manager stored the truck that was leaving in one shared variable named
currentlyWasActiveTruck. If the next truck became full before the previous
truck returned, that shared reference was overwritten.

This could enqueue the wrong truck, add the same truck to the queue more than
once, or start a queue-position movement while the truck was still following
its unload route. Two coroutines would then write to the same Transform and the
truck could stop, jitter, or remain at a waypoint.

New movement ownership
----------------------
LiftTruckManager now runs one complete trip for the exact truck passed into
MoveActiveTruck:

1. Mark that truck as in transit.
2. Remove that exact truck from the playable queue.
3. Activate the next queued truck.
4. Follow the outgoing waypoints.
5. Unload the boxes.
6. Follow the return waypoints.
7. Add that exact truck once to the back of the queue.

The HashSet named trucksInTransit is a lock. It prevents two full events in the
same frame from starting two route coroutines for one truck.

Queue movement safety in LiftTruck
----------------------------------
queueMovementCoroutine stores the optional coroutine that moves a waiting
truck between queue positions. Starting a new queue movement cancels the old
one. Starting a waypoint route also cancels queue movement first. Therefore
only one movement routine controls a truck Transform at a time.

Additional guards
-----------------
- Duplicate AddBox callbacks for the same GameObject are ignored.
- A truck cannot appear twice in the queue.
- Null waypoints are skipped safely.
- Movement uses a small positive minimum speed, preventing an accidental zero
  Inspector speed from producing an endless movement loop.
- The truck snaps exactly to each final target after entering its tolerance.
- Every truck in the waiting queue is restored to its original parked
  orientation facing world +X after a route.

What was not changed
--------------------
Truck speed values, waypoint positions, capacity, box appearance, scoring,
matching, and box-transfer rules were not changed.
